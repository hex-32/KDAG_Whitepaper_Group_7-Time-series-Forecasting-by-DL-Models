# 1. Introduction

## 1.1 Background

Time series forecasting is the process of predicting future values from historical sequential data. It is a fundamental task in predictive analytics with applications spanning finance, healthcare, energy, transportation, manufacturing, retail, and climate science. Accurate forecasting supports informed decision-making, efficient resource allocation, and risk management.

## 1.2 Motivation

Traditional **univariate**(can predict only one value) statistical models such as **ARIMA(AutoRegressive Integrated Moving Average)** and **SARIMA(Seasonal AutoRegressive Integrated Moving Average)** have long been used for forecasting but often struggle with the nonlinear, high-dimensional, and large-scale nature of modern time series data. Recent advances in deep learning have enabled models to learn complex temporal patterns directly from data, leading to significant improvements in forecasting performance across a wide range of applications.

## 1.3 Objectives

This whitepaper aims to provide a concise overview of deep learning approaches for time series forecasting, covering their evolution, core architectures, benchmark datasets, evaluation methods, current research challenges, and emerging trends. It also highlights the strengths and limitations of modern forecasting techniques and identifies promising directions for future research.
