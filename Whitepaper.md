# Time Series Forecasting by Deep Learning Models
    This whitepaper provides a comprehensive overview of time series forecasting using deep learning models. It covers the fundamentals, evolution, and various architectures of deep learning models, along with datasets, benchmarking, current challenges, applications across industries, comparative analysis, and future research opportunities.


## Abstract

    Time series forecasting is essential for predictive analytics across domains such as finance, healthcare, energy, and transportation. While traditional statistical models perform well on simple datasets, they often struggle with the nonlinear and complex patterns present in real-world time series. Deep learning has emerged as a powerful alternative, enabling more accurate modeling of temporal dependencies through architectures such as LSTMs, Temporal Convolutional Networks (TCNs), Transformers, and recent foundation models.

    This whitepaper surveys the evolution of deep learning techniques for time series forecasting, highlighting their architectures, strengths, limitations, and practical applications. It further discusses commonly used benchmark datasets, evaluation metrics, and key research challenges, including long-horizon forecasting, distribution shift, uncertainty estimation, and computational efficiency. Finally, the paper explores emerging research directions and outlines opportunities for developing more robust, interpretable, and scalable forecasting models.
